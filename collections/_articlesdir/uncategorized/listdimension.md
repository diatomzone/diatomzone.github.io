## top items

my best purchases and other must-haves!

- backpack with suede bottom (brand: jansport)
  - none of that peeling fake leather nonsense (like my previous backpack). while i'm not using it every day for school anymore, it's traveled all over lasting years and years, with only the seams at the top starting to come apart! i even put it through the wash {{site.data.emojis.zenigata}}
- huion screen drawing tablet (model: kamvas pro 16)
  - battery-less pen, etched glass to emulate paper texture, massive real estate, and excellent performance, all for a decidedly non-cintiq price! after years of use, the only sign of wear is on the cord -- the plastic wire housing has torn at a connection point, despite not being particularly roughhoused... so i duct taped it.
- metal body ballpoint pen (brand: ohto gs01)
  - the main issue with click-style ballpoints, i've found, is how the gap between the metal pen tip and the housing allows for a small degree of wiggle, slightly reducing accuracy/smoothness. this pen's sturdy construction minimizes that gap! also, the replaceable cartridge has lasted me years of normal use, i JUST changed it.
- grocery store reusable water bottle (brand: unknown, the label's worn off)
  - a basic stainless steel vaccum-walled bottle with a narrow, screw-on top. i only ever fill it with water, so i've never had issues with mold or discoloration. the textured finish is wearing off, revealing plain steel, but it's otherwise in perfect condition after 6 years of regular use! a slim size that fits well in bags.
- mini swiss army knife (brand: victorinox)
  - a keepsake from my youth, still useful to this day! the scissors come in handy for clipping things and opening tough wrappers more often than i expect. also, i've gotten it confiscated by TSA twice (jumping through hoops to belatedly stow it in a checked bag), so i no longer fly with it. (it's gotten through security way more often than not...)
- monocular (brand: ???)
  - an ancient(?) 1/2 binocular from my grandma! it's really small with good clarity and ease of use. i typically bring this out to project solar eclipses onto a  sheet of black paper for safe viewing -- point the eye side at a dark surface, let the sun shine through, focus the lens, and you have a crisp, gorgeous image that's WAY larger than a pinhole camera's!
- pentel hi-polymer eraser (brand: pentel)
  - from my childhood to present, these are the best, no contest. infallible even through time -- i have ones from perhaps 10+ years ago that still work and feel like new! no greasy smearing here.
- durable canvas tote bag (brand: ???)
  - a random garage find, it's strong, lightweight, spacious, compactable, and washable! no zippers that jam or break, which is what killed my previous bags. you can even store a second tote bag inside for an adaptable grocery run.
- stainless steel 18-inch ruler (brand: art alternatives)
  - precise and nearly indestructible. many papers and crafts are longer than 12 inches. pairs well with an xacto knife for precision cutting!
- 2mm lead mechanical pencil (brand: daiso)
  - this "clutch pencil" was another class material that turned out to be a keeper! the durable lead and line weight variation a regular pencil without the sharpening hassle.
- pocket magnifying glass (brand: ???)
  - lightweight plastic rectangle that stores like a slider phone. features 3 lenses with varying levels of magnification: 30x (nice big lens, perfect for phone camera pics), 60x (small and difficult to use with a phone camera), and 90x (basically unusable due to the tiny size and depth of field). a must-have for plant and bug inspection!
- dog poop bag
  - i keep one folded up tinystyle in my bag and it's been surprisingly handy! confidently bag anything wet or gross -- snotty tissues, banana peels, or even lend it to a dog in need!
- yakuza 0
  - value-wise, this is undeniable. $5 USD steam sale and look where it's gotten me {{site.data.emojis.reigen}}
